# Research track profile

**Positioning:** A bioengineer with hands-on experience in bioprocess development, protein purification and
organ-on-chip platforms, who also understands how R&D decisions affect a product's path to market.

**Resume file:** kb/resumes/resume_research.pdf

**Research interests:** biologics and peptide therapeutics (GLP-1), drug delivery, microphysiological systems for
disease modeling and drug screening, cardiovascular disease, immune-epithelial co-culture and bone biology,
translational research.

## Skills
- Lab and bioprocess: downstream purification (ion-exchange chromatography, HPLC); process optimization and
  troubleshooting for recombinant proteins and peptides; protein characterization (SDS-PAGE, UV-Vis, FTIR, purity
  analysis); GMP-compliant workflows and scale-up awareness; mammalian cell culture and aseptic technique;
  3D co-culture; microscopy and image-based analysis
- Engineering and platforms: microfluidic device design, organ-on-chip development, disease modeling for drug
  screening, CAD (Fusion 360, SolidWorks), biomedical design and prototyping
- Research and analysis: experimental design and reproducibility, data analysis and biostatistics (Python, R),
  computational modeling of physiological systems, literature and clinical-trial review, lab documentation,
  regulatory literacy for biologics and devices
- Tools: HPLC and chromatography systems, SDS-PAGE, UV-Vis, FTIR, microscopy; Excel, GraphPad Prism, ImageJ/Fiji,
  MATLAB; ClinicalTrials.gov, CTRI, PubMed

## Strengths
1. A measurable lab result: +90% recombinant GLP-1 yield at an industrial manufacturer.
2. Range across the pipeline: device and platform R&D, bioprocess and manufacturing, clinical development.
3. Translational mindset: understands commercial and regulatory constraints.
4. Fast learner across research institutes, Indian pharma and a US startup.
